3 Famous World Landmarks

  1. Eiffel Tower – Paris, France

    The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in the world. It was built in 1889 and is located in Paris, France. It is known for its beautiful iron architecture and attracts millions of tourists every year. It is also considered a symbol of France.

  2. Great Wall of China – China

    The Great Wall of China is one of the most famous historical landmarks in the world. It was built to protect China from invasions and attacks. It stretches across thousands of kilometres and is known for its amazing architecture and historical importance. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  3. Taj Mahal – Agra, India

    The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful historical monuments in the world. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Completed in 1653, it is famous for its white marble architecture and beautiful gardens. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
